APPOINTMENT NOTES

My health story

A patient-prepared summary to support a conversation with a qualified clinician.

1. Main concern

Write the main symptom or concern in your own words.

2. Timeline and pattern

When it began, how often it occurs, and any changes you have noticed.

3. Other relevant details

Cycle details, pain, energy, medicines, previous care, or daily-life impact.

4. Questions for my clinician

  • What could be contributing to these symptoms?
  • What next steps would you recommend, and why?
  • When should I follow up or seek urgent care?
This template is not a diagnosis, medical advice, or a clinical score. Please review all information for accuracy before sharing.
