CARE COMES FIRST

Your safety matters.

HEARD helps you prepare for a conversation. It cannot assess emergencies or tell you what condition you have.

!

When to seek urgent help

If you have severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek urgent medical care rather than waiting to finish this tool.

  • Very heavy bleeding, especially with weakness, dizziness, or fainting
  • Severe or sudden abdominal or pelvic pain
  • Fainting, trouble breathing, chest pain, or feeling seriously unwell
  • Any symptom that makes you feel you may be in immediate danger

This is not a complete list. If you are worried about your safety, contact local emergency services or a qualified clinician.
