Privacy by design.

Your health information is personal. Here is what this frontend demo does and does not do.

⌁

No server submission

This static frontend demo does not send chat messages to a backend or external AI service.

▣

Demo interactions only

Some screens use example content. Do not treat it as saved medical records or a secure health account.

✧

Use fictional data

Until a secure backend and privacy protections are implemented, use sample information only.

Before using a real person's information

A production health app needs a clear privacy notice, informed consent, appropriate access controls, secure transmission and storage, deletion procedures, and careful review of applicable laws. This demo does not provide those protections.

HEARD is designed to help prepare for a healthcare conversation. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional care.
